# Themed Claims

Status: active
Content type: INTERVIEW (long, multi-theme)

Theme headings group related claim-bullets. Combines scannability of claim-bullets with thematic structure for long multi-topic content.

## Template

```
## [Main heading for the entire video — one sentence, informative]

**TL;DR**: [1 sentence synthesis]

### [Theme]
- **[Claim]**: detail fragment
- **[Claim]**: detail fragment

### [Theme]
- **[Claim]**: detail fragment

## Hidden Gems
- **[Claim]**: tangent or anecdote outside the main themes
```

## Example

```
## Why structural barriers outweigh individual effort in closing the gender gap

**TL;DR**: Systemic fixes like equal parental leave and eliminating confidence penalties matter more than asking women to "lean in" harder.

### Workplace flexibility is necessary but not sufficient
- **Equal parental leave changes the equation**: Facebook's four-month leave for both genders, taken by men, directly freed partners to stay in workforce
- **Domestic labor imbalance is the real blocker**: women in dual-income households do 2× housework and 3× childcare

### Confidence deficit drives attrition more than skill deficit
- **Women attribute success to luck, men to ability**: makes women less likely to raise their hand for promotion
- **Women who dropped engineering had higher grades than men who dropped**: the difference was zero close role models in the field

## Hidden Gems
- **"Likability penalty" disappears when criteria are written down first**: studies show evaluators hold women to unwritten standards that evaporate once rubrics are made explicit before review
```

## Rules

- Main heading: ## level, one sentence, informative
- TL;DR: mandatory, never remove, one sentence synthesis
- Theme headings: ### level, 4-6 max
- 2-3 claim-bullets per theme
- Bold label: complete claim sentence, front-loaded
- After colon: 1-2 short sentences with evidence or mechanism
- Use for long interviews/panels with clearly shifting topics
- If themes are not distinct, fall back to claim-bullets (flat)
- Hidden Gems: end with `## Hidden Gems` if the transcript contains valuable tangents, anecdotes, or details outside the main themes. 1-3 claim-bullets. Omit only if nothing qualifies.

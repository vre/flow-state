# Claim Bullets

Status: active
Content type: EDUCATIONAL (broad topic)

Flat bullet list where each bold label is a claim. Combines scannability of flat-bullets with informativeness of claim-first. No hierarchy, no sections.

## Template

```
- **[Claim sentence]**: supporting detail as fragment, one or two short sentences max
- **[Claim sentence]**: supporting detail with numbers or mechanism
```

## Example

```
- **Earth radiates back exactly as much energy as it receives**: conservation of energy requires balance — otherwise Earth would heat or cool continuously
- **Entropy measures how spread out energy is**: low entropy = concentrated, able to do work; high entropy = dispersed, unavailable
- **Heat flowing cold→hot is statistically improbable, not forbidden**: at 10²⁵ atoms the probability is effectively zero
```

## Rules

- Bold label: complete claim sentence, front-loaded, informative enough to read without the detail
- After colon: fragments or short sentences — evidence, numbers, mechanism. Not full paragraphs.
- Flat list only — NO ### headings, NO hierarchy, NO sections
- 7-10 bullets for normal content, fewer for short transcripts
- Use when topic covers multiple distinct claims that don't fit into 1-2 what-why-how units
- End with `## Hidden Gems` section if the transcript contains valuable tangents, anecdotes, or details outside the main argument. 1-3 bullets, same claim-bullet format. Omit only if nothing qualifies.

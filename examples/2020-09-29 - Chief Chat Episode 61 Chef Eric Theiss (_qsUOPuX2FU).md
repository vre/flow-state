## Summary: Chef Eric Theiss on keto cooking, career shortcuts, and live fried chicken demo

**TL;DR**: Eric Theiss (America's Comfort Chef) traces his path from dishwasher to TV host, explains his low-glycemic eating framework, then demos keto fried chicken — dry-wet-dry with pork rind crumbs in an air fryer, Jersey hot sauce finish.

### Keto Fried Chicken with Jersey Hot Sauce

**Prerequisites**: air fryer (10-qt vortex or equivalent), skinless boneless chicken thighs, almond flour, eggs, heavy cream, plain pork rinds (blended to crumbs)

1. Mise en place in a line: raw chicken → almond flour → egg/cream wash → seasoned pork rind crumbs → holding pan
2. Dredge in almond flour — makes wet coating stick; do not skip
3. Dip in egg + heavy cream
4. Press into pork rind crumbs seasoned with paprika, onion powder, turmeric, cumin, parmesan
5. Refrigerate 15 min — hydrates crust, prevents it falling off
6. Spray air fryer rack with olive oil
7. Air fry until cooked through and golden
8. Jersey hot sauce: melt butter, add cumin, cayenne, honey, tomato paste, vinegar; heat to combine — salty + sweet + sour + hot
9. Drizzle over chicken; serve with broccolini roasted at 325°F ~20 min

**Result**: Full crunch, no flour, no deep fry oil; works on shrimp, pork cutlets, chicken breast

## Hidden Gems

- **Ravioli lasagna shortcut**: layer frozen ravioli with ricotta, sauce, mozzarella, bake ~1 hour — skips tedious pasta-sheet layering
- **Roast broccolini past the point it looks burnt**: concentrates sweetness; the threshold is intentional
- **Dry-wet-dry is grandmother technique, not chef trick**: predates air fryers; maps perfectly to air fryer constraints
